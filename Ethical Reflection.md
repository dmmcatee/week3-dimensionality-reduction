Reflection on how dimensionality reduction could introduce bias
- Dimensionality reduction can introduce bias because features with lower variance or representing smaller groups may be reduced or removed entirely, causing important patters or minority groups to be underrepresented.

How visualization choices might affect interpretation
- Visualization choices like axis scalin, colors, and layout can exaggerate or hide patterns, leading viewers to draw conclusions that may not fully reflect the original data.

How diverse perspectives improve fairness in feature selection
- Diverse perspectives help ensure important features are not overlooked and reduce the risk of selecting variables that unfairly disadvantage certain groups.
